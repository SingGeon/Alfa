How it works

From raw data to a confidence-scored signal, fully automated.

  1. 01 📡

    Data collection

    Live price and candles from Binance (CoinGecko/Etherscan fallback), DeFi TVL from DeFiLlama, and news from 10 crypto RSS sources.

  2. 02 🧮

    Indicators + sentiment

    RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands and the buy/sell ratio, combined with a VADER sentiment score for every news article.

  3. 03 🤖

    Per-interval ML model

    A scikit-learn ensemble trained separately for each interval (15m/1h/4h/1d/1w), auto-recalibrated as new data arrives.

  4. 04 🎯

    Prediction + confidence

    An estimated price with a confidence interval, a Buy/Sell/Wait signal with its own backtested accuracy history, and a calibrated confidence score — updated continuously.

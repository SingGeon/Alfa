🧬 Model population

The new model is a population of small models that live, die and have children on the real ETH price history. Here is everything about it: who died, when, where the market was and why, and what changed from generation to generation.

At a glance

The money: 100 € each

Every model starts with 100 € (virtual) and trades ETH with it: long, short or out, paying Binance's 0.1% fee on every trade and a daily tax of 0.05 € (0.15 € on days its money sits out of the market), so it must earn at least that. Money is its life: below 10 € it dies, and the slowest grower is replaced by a child of the rich. The fund is the population's vote trading 100 € the same way, next to simply buying ETH with 100 € and holding it.

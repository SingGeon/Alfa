💼 Strategy fund

Where ETH goes next can't be predicted (about 50% over nine years), but how much it will move can. This fund trades on that: a population of strategies, each mixing three pieces, evolves on the real history and the fund follows the best of them. Everything below was decided only from the past, candle by candle, with Binance's 0.1% fee on every trade.

Only on 4h and 1d: there it beat buy & hold on years it had never seen. On 1h the fees ate the profit; 15m is still to be tested.

What selection picked

Every month each strategy is scored on the years before (money after fees, minus a penalty for its worst drop); the weakest are replaced by children of the strongest, and the fund holds the average of the best four, in three independent populations. Nobody sets these by hand: this is how the best strategies' genes moved.
